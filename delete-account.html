Delete Your Account

Permanently remove your account and all associated data

You can delete your Clafy account directly from within the app at any time. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

1

Open the Clafy app and make sure you are logged in.

2

Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.

3

Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete Account.

4

Confirm the deletion when prompted. Your account will be permanently deleted immediately.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of your data from our database, including your email address, username, health profile preferences, avoided ingredients, and scan history. This cannot be reversed.

Need help?

If you are unable to access your account or need assistance deleting your data, contact us at info@clafy.fit and we will delete your account manually.
